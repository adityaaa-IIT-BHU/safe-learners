Understand how Safe Learners works

Overview:

Safe Learners is built for independent instructors, and we know it will benefit your business too! What makes Safe Learners so great?

  1. Reach more learner drivers - we provide a platform for driving instructors to showcase their services to a wider audience. Safe Learners can increase your visibility and attract potential clients to your business.
  2. Convenient scheduling - we offer a user-friendly, real-time scheduling system, allowing instructors to manage their availability and schedule lessons with ease, benefiting both instructors and learners.
  3. Expert marketing support - we assist driving instructors with marketing by featuring their profiles on the platform, making it easier for learners to find and connect with them.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Safe Learners™ is the trademark of AM & BN Group Pty Ltd trading as Safe Learners Driver Education.

We aim for all bookings made through the Safe Learners platform to proceed smoothly, but we understand that unexpected issues may arise.

If a booking does not meet your expectations and you wish to request a refund, please contact our Support Team.

We strive to offer fair refunds for learners. To check if you're eligible, see the guidelines below.

Any decision to grant a refund is at Safe Learners’ sole discretion, but a refund will generally be agreed if you meet the eligibility criteria below.

When Can You Request a Refund?

You may qualify for a refund in situations such as:

  • No driving instructors are available in your area.
  • No instructors are available for bookings within the next 4 weeks.
  • A duplicate payment has been made.
  • You wish to exchange your booking credits, such as: Converting a test package credit into lesson hours, or swapping driving lesson credits for a driving test package.
  • You have completed over 10 hours of lessons and no longer need your remaining credits.
  • Your instructor did not arrive for the scheduled lesson.
